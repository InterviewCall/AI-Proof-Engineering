### Section 7 — 10-Phase Curriculum

**Eyebrow:**
The Curriculum

**Headline:**
The Curriculum, Phase by Phase

**Subheadline:**
Ten phases, each ending in something you build and someone reviews.

**Cards / Components:**

01 · Engineering Mindset & Baseline — A baseline read on how you approach a problem, structure code, and debug when something breaks.
02 · Core CS & Problem Solving — Data structures, algorithms, and enough timed practice that a hard problem doesn't rattle you.
03 · AI-Assisted Engineering Workflow — Use AI to draft and debug faster, and learn to catch it when it's confidently wrong.
04 · Backend, APIs & Databases — Design an API, choose a schema, implement auth, and handle the failure cases.
05 · System Design Fundamentals — HLD, LLD, caching, queues, and the trade-offs behind each choice.
06 · Machine Coding & Debugging — Turn a spec into working code under a time limit, then explain what you'd change with more of it.
07 · AI Product Building — Integrate a model into a real feature, including what happens when it gets something wrong.
08 · Production Readiness — Deploy it, monitor it, and figure out what broke when it does.
09 · Interview Readiness & Career Positioning — Mock interviews, resume review, and practice explaining your own work under questions.
10 · Capstone Proof & Final Review — Finish and defend a complete piece of work, end to end.

**Expanded phase (default selected: Phase 03):**

**Phase 03 — AI-Assisted Engineering Workflow**
You'll use AI to draft code, debug, and refactor faster. Every exercise also asks you to explain what you kept from its output, what you rewrote, and why.
Covers: giving AI enough context to be useful, prompting for an explanation instead of just an answer, debugging with AI, refactoring with AI, reviewing generated code line by line.
You'll produce: a debugging write-up and a code review showing what you changed and why.

### Section 18 — FAQ (Desktop — 7 questions)

**Q1. What is the AI-Proof Engineer Program?**
A program for software engineers that covers fundamentals, AI-assisted workflows, system design, and a capstone project you build and defend. It's split into three tracks based on experience.

**Q2. Is this designed for working professionals?**
Yes — most people join while working full-time. `[CONFIRM TIME COMMITMENT]`: we'll publish the expected weekly hours before enrollment opens.

**Q3. How are Foundation, Growth, and Leadership different?**
Foundation is for 0–2 years of experience and focuses on fundamentals. Growth is for 2–5 years and goes deeper into system design and AI workflows. Leadership is for 5+ years and focuses on architecture, trade-offs, and senior-level interviews.

**Q4. Does using AI in the program mean I'll end up depending on it?**
Phase 3 has you use AI to draft and debug code, but every output gets reviewed and tested before it counts, so the habit of checking your own work stays intact.

**Q5. What practice, mocks, and reviews are included?**
Problem sets, debugging exercises, system design labs, and AI-workflow exercises, each with mentor feedback. Mock interviews cover DSA, machine coding, and system design. Your capstone gets reviewed before it's considered done.

**Q6. What will I actually walk away with?**
A repository, an architecture write-up, a recorded walkthrough, and documentation, built around a direction that fits your track — a backend system, an AI workflow project, or a system design case study, for example.

**Q7. How do admissions and fee counselling work?**
Book a call. Someone on the team goes over the track that fits you, the current fee plan, and payment options. `[CONFIRM COHORT MODEL]`: we'll confirm here whether enrollment runs continuously or in cohorts.
